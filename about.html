About Vandrick Kel

Who Is Vandrick Kel?

Vandrick Kel is a Filipino TikTok star, actor, dancer, model and digital content creator from Olongapo City, Zambales, Philippines. He is recognized for his presence across digital platforms, particularly through dance covers, short-form entertainment content, lifestyle material and creative performances.

His work brings together digital entertainment, dance, fashion, visual content and acting. Through social media and creative projects, Vandrick Kel continues to develop his identity as a young Filipino entertainer and digital creator.

Early Life & Background

Vandrick Kel was born on December 10, 2008 and raised in Olongapo City, Zambales, Philippines. His full name is J-cob Rosejos Mendigorin, while Vandrick Kel serves as his stage and screen name.

Growing up, he developed an interest in music, performance arts and creative camera work. His involvement in school activities and dance helped strengthen his interest in performing and creating visual content.

As the eldest among his siblings, Vandrick Kel has also developed personal ambitions connected to his education, family and future. His interests extend beyond entertainment, including Psychology and Fashion Design.

The Beginning of His Digital Journey

Vandrick Kel began building his digital presence through short-form content, particularly on TikTok. His early creative work focused on dance performances, lip-sync routines, trending challenges, lifestyle snippets and viral audio-based videos.

Through his consistent interest in digital entertainment and performance, he gradually developed an audience and established himself as a young internet personality in the Philippines.
