Vandrick Kel Biography

Vandrick Kel is a Filipino TikTok star, content creator, model, dancer and actor from Olongapo City, Zambales, Philippines. Recognized for his dance covers, engaging short-form videos, creative performances and growing digital presence, he is building his career across social media and entertainment.

Born on December 10, 2008, Vandrick Kel is the stage and screen name of J-cob Rosejos Mendigorin. His creative journey has been shaped by his interest in music, dance, performance, visual content and digital entertainment.

Early Life & Background

Vandrick Kel was born and raised in Olongapo City, Zambales, Philippines. He is the eldest among his siblings and developed an early interest in music, performance arts and creative camera work.

During his school years, he participated in school events and dance activities while balancing academics with his growing interest in digital content creation and entertainment.

Digital Career

Vandrick Kel began his digital career through TikTok, where his content has included dance performances, lip-sync routines, trending dance challenges, lifestyle snippets and viral audio clips.
